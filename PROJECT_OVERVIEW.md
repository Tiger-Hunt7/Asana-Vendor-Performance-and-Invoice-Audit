# Project Overview

## Business request

Management requested an evidence-based review after a vendor asserted that more than $40,000 remained unpaid.

## Finding

The audit found **$0 payable for completed work remained unpaid**. The $23,685 categorized as denied related to work that was not performed or did not satisfy documented requirements. It was therefore not an unpaid obligation.

## Analytical value

The project converted exported Asana activity into a structured record of service periods, task completion, inspections, open items, and financial classifications. It gave management a detailed basis for the organization's position and exposed discrepancies that required reconciliation.

## Scope

- 147 Asana tasks
- 124 completed and 23 open
- 45 failed inspections
- 21 undated tasks
- 91 itemized financial records
- $139,948 reported paid
- $23,685 denied for work not performed or requirements not met
- $163,633 total itemized activity reviewed

## Limitation

Asana is a project-management record, not an authoritative financial ledger. The analysis used it as operational evidence and flagged payment classifications for confirmation against the financial system and supporting invoice documentation.
